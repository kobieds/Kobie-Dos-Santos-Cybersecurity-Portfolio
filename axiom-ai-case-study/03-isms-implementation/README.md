# Axiom AI Technologies — ISMS Implementation

## Overview

This workstream demonstrates the design and implementation of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) for **Axiom AI Technologies**, a fictional B2B AI/ML SaaS organization with approximately 180 employees.

The ISMS implementation builds upon the cybersecurity risk assessment and security controls assessment completed in the preceding workstreams.

The objective is to demonstrate how an organization can translate identified risks and control requirements into a structured information security management system aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**.

---

## Objectives

The ISMS implementation is designed to demonstrate the ability to:

* Define an appropriate ISMS scope
* Establish information security governance
* Develop an overarching Information Security Policy
* Define measurable information security objectives
* Determine the applicability of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls
* Connect identified risks to applicable controls
* Establish roles and responsibilities for ISMS management
* Document the relationship between risks, controls, policies, and remediation
* Establish a foundation for continual improvement

---

## Relationship to Previous Workstreams

The Axiom AI case study is designed as a continuous GRC program rather than a collection of unrelated projects.

The ISMS implementation uses outputs from the previous workstreams as inputs.

### Risk Management

The Risk Management workstream established:

* Asset inventory
* Cybersecurity risk register
* Risk treatment plans
* Inherent and residual risk considerations
* Risk ownership and target dates

### Security Controls Assessment

The Security Controls Assessment evaluated 40 security controls against:

* ISO/IEC 27001:2022
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
* NIST SP 800-53

The assessment documented control effectiveness, findings, remediation activities, and validation status.

### ISMS Implementation

The ISMS workstream uses these outputs to establish:

**Risk → Control → Policy → Objective → Treatment → Continual Improvement**

---

## ISMS Framework

The implementation is primarily aligned with:

* **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**
* **ISO/IEC 27002:2022**
* **ISO/IEC 27005** risk management principles

Supporting cybersecurity concepts are informed by:

* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
* NIST SP 800-53

ISO/IEC 27001 provides the primary management-system framework for this workstream.

## ISMS Lifecycle

The Axiom AI ISMS is designed around a continual improvement lifecycle:

```text
Organizational Context
        ↓
ISMS Scope
        ↓
Risk Assessment
        ↓
Control Assessment
        ↓
Statement of Applicability
        ↓
Policies & Objectives
        ↓
Risk Treatment & Remediation
        ↓
Performance Monitoring
        ↓
Management Review
        ↓
Continual Improvement
```

This lifecycle demonstrates that the ISMS is not a static collection of policies. It is a management system that continuously evaluates information security risks, controls, performance, and improvement opportunities.

---

## Key Governance Principles

The ISMS implementation follows several core principles:

### Risk-Based Decision Making

Information security decisions should be informed by documented risks and their potential impact on Axiom's information assets, customers, and business operations.

### Defined Accountability

Information security responsibilities should be assigned to appropriate individuals and functions rather than being treated as the responsibility of a single security team.

### Control Effectiveness

The presence of a documented control does not automatically demonstrate effectiveness. Controls should be supported by appropriate evidence, testing, monitoring, and validation.

### Continual Improvement

The ISMS should evolve as Axiom's technology environment, business operations, threats, regulatory obligations, and risk profile change.

### Evidence-Based Governance

ISMS decisions and control implementation should be supported by documented evidence that can be reviewed by management, auditors, and other authorized stakeholders.

## Portfolio Scope and Limitations

Axiom AI Technologies is a fictional organization created for portfolio demonstration purposes.

This workstream does not represent an actual ISO/IEC 27001 certification project and does not constitute a certification audit.

The artifacts are designed to demonstrate practical GRC, cybersecurity risk management, and ISMS implementation capabilities using simulated organizational information.

No real credentials, confidential information, customer data, production systems, or proprietary organizational information are included.
