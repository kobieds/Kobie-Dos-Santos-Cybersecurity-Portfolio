# Management Review

## Overview

This section documents the management review process for the Axiom AI Technologies Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The management review provides executive oversight of the ISMS and evaluates whether the organization's information-security governance, risk-management, compliance, and operational controls remain suitable, adequate, and effective.

The management review brings together outputs from the previous Axiom AI case-study projects and converts those findings into management-level decisions, priorities, and improvement actions.

## Purpose

The purpose of the management review is to:

* Evaluate the continuing suitability of the ISMS.
* Evaluate the adequacy and effectiveness of information-security controls.
* Review significant information-security risks and treatment activities.
* Review compliance and assessment results.
* Evaluate progress against security objectives.
* Review security incidents, vulnerabilities, and remediation activities.
* Review business continuity and disaster-recovery readiness.
* Evaluate third-party and supplier-security risks.
* Identify resource requirements.
* Identify opportunities for continual improvement.
* Establish management decisions and corrective actions.

## Management Review Methodology

The management review follows a structured process:

```text
Collect ISMS Inputs
       ↓
Evaluate Performance
       ↓
Review Risks & Compliance
       ↓
Identify Significant Issues
       ↓
Evaluate Control Effectiveness
       ↓
Identify Improvement Opportunities
       ↓
Make Management Decisions
       ↓
Assign Actions & Owners
       ↓
Set Target Dates
       ↓
Track and Validate Actions
```

Management decisions should be evidence-based and aligned with organizational risk.

## Management Decision Categories

Management decisions should generally fall into one or more of the following categories:

### Accept

Management accepts the identified residual risk without additional treatment.

### Treat

Additional controls or remediation activities are required.

### Transfer

Risk is transferred through contractual, insurance, supplier, or other appropriate mechanisms.

### Avoid

The activity creating unacceptable risk is discontinued or materially changed.

### Monitor

The issue remains within acceptable tolerance but requires continued monitoring.

### Improve

A process or control is functioning but should be strengthened.

## Escalation

Issues should be escalated to appropriate management when they:

* Exceed organizational risk tolerance.
* Create significant compliance exposure.
* Remain unresolved beyond an approved deadline.
* Affect critical business services.
* Affect confidentiality, integrity, or availability of important information.
* Indicate recurring control failure.
* Require significant financial or staffing resources.
* Require acceptance of significant residual risk.

## Management Review Frequency

The management review should occur at least annually.

Additional reviews may be initiated following significant events such as:

* Major security incidents
* Significant organizational changes
* Major technology changes
* Material changes in regulatory requirements
* Significant changes to the threat environment
* Major audit findings
* Significant changes in business strategy
* Material changes to the ISMS scope
